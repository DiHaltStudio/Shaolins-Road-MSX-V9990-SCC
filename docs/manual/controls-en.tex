The controls are those of the arcade. The keyboard, the cursor keys and joystick 1 work at the same time.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{\tAction} & \textbf{\tKeyboard} & \textbf{\tJoystick}\\
\midrule
Move left / right & \key{$\leftarrow$} \key{$\rightarrow$} & stick left / right\\
Up: jump to the floor above & \key{$\uparrow$} & stick up\\
Down: drop to the floor below & \key{$\downarrow$} & stick down\\
Kick & \key{SPACE} or \key{Z} & button A\\
Jump & \key{M} or \key{X} & button B\\
Start, hard mode (title screen) & \key{1}, \key{SPACE} & button A\\
Start, medium mode (title screen) & \key{2} & button B\\
Letters of your initials & \key{$\leftarrow$} \key{$\rightarrow$}, kick to accept & stick, button A\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Notes.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The stick behaves like the arcade lever. If you reverse direction without passing through the centre, the game gives the stick one neutral frame so that the hero turns round instead of walking backwards.
\item Kick while you jump to land a \emph{jump kick}, worth more points.
\item \key{SPACE}, button A and button B start the game only from the title screen and the demo. During the game, and when you enter your initials, they only kick and jump.
\item The two modes are one-player games. \textbf{Hard} is the arcade's tough setting; \textbf{medium} gives the enemies less speed and aggression, and still gets harder with every stage.
\item There is no pause key and no reset key, as in the arcade.
\end{itemize}
