\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Start-up message.} On the MSX's own screen, \texttt{KONAMI 1985 / DIHALT STUDIO 2026 / V9990 found !} for two seconds.
\item \textbf{DiHalt logo.} Shown for four seconds; it cannot be skipped.
\item \textbf{Cover.} The full-screen illustration. Press any key or any joystick direction or button to continue (it also goes on by itself after ten seconds).
\item \textbf{Title screen.} \texttt{PRESS START}, \texttt{1 HARD 2 MEDIUM} and the extra-life information. Press \key{1}, \key{SPACE} or the joystick button \key{A} to start in \textbf{hard} mode, or \key{2} or the joystick button \key{B} to start in \textbf{medium} mode. If you wait, the game plays a demonstration and shows the high-score table, then returns to the title.
\item \textbf{The game}: five stages, then the same five again, a little harder, and so on.
\end{enumerate}

When you lose your last life the game shows \texttt{GAME OVER}. If your score is among the best eight you enter your three initials; then the title screen comes back.
